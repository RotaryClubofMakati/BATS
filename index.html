Introduction
Launched in February 1988, the Books Across the Seas (BATS) program is a testament to the Rotary Club of Makati's commitment to supporting less privileged communities by partnering with other Rotary Clubs, primarily in District 3830, for book donation and distribution initiatives. Administered by the Rotary Club of Makati (RCM) and funded by the Makati Rotary Club Foundation, Inc. (MRCFI), with support from Philippine National Bank and Rex Bookstore, these guidelines serve as a Memorandum of Agreement between RCM and requesting Rotary Clubs or Districts.

Request Guidelines

  1. All book requests are subject to approval by the BATS Chairman.
  2. Requested books are subject to availability and are handled on a first-come, first-served basis.
  3. A token donation of ₱3.00 per book is required for project expenses.
  4. Books are not for resale and should not be used for political purposes.
  5. Reporting is required within 30 days after pickup, including photos of the turnover.
Only the requestor's own submission result is shown. Inventory quantities and other requests are private.
